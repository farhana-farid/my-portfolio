========================================================================
HOW TO UPLOAD TO TIINY HOST (tiiny.host) IN 3 SIMPLE STEPS
========================================================================

Ready-to-upload ZIP file:
File: bbb.zip

STEP 1: Open Tiiny Host
-----------------------
Go to your browser and visit:
https://tiiny.host

STEP 2: Drag and Drop the ZIP file
----------------------------------
- Take the file 'bbb.zip' from this folder (or from your Desktop).
- Drag and drop it into the 'Upload your file' area on tiiny.host.

STEP 3: Name your site and Publish
----------------------------------
- Enter a link name (e.g. farhana-portfolio).
- Click 'Publish'.
- Your 3D interactive portfolio website is now live on the internet!
